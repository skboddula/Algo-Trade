This is **not** evidence of an edge and does not support live arming. It is
also not a reason to loosen or widen stops: on this day the tight stops were

## Opening-range mechanics

The three opening entries were not independent reads; they were the same
bearish macro stack, released symbol-by-symbol as each stale gate cleared:

- **NIFTY 22600 PE**: day open 132.5, first-minute high 200.85, 09:16 low
  154.45; entry 178.13. The strong 16 was a stale pre-open artefact; the
  first fresh score was 11.
- **FINNIFTY 24450 PE**: 09:16 bar 189.75 → 141.95; entry 171.02.
- **BANKNIFTY 54600 PE**: 09:16 low 336.55, 09:18 low 301.35; entry 366.52.

The common pattern is **gate-clear chasing**: the system waits for indicators
to become valid, then enters immediately at whatever premium the opening
price discovery offers. On this day that was the most volatile bar of the
session.

## What didn't work / open risks

### A. Opening gate-clear chase

The entries landed in the 09:16 bar, the widest of the day. This is a
structural timing issue, not a scoring failure: the score was only released
when the stale gate cleared, and the first fresh evaluation was acted on
immediately. The 09:15–09:16 strong NIFTY 16 was a stale artefact; by the
time data was fresh, the score was 11. A warm-up window is the natural
hypothesis to test.

### B. Paper fills are not a fill model

Paper entries fill at the signal-time option LTP + 0.1%; live entries are
**MARKET** orders. In today's opening bars, the next-bar open was sometimes
better and sometimes worse than the paper fill: FINNIFTY's 09:16 bar opened
**11% above** the paper entry, while NIFTY and BANKNIFTY's next bars opened
below it. The paper ledger is not systematically optimistic — it is simply
not a fill model. A replay that fills at the next 1-minute bar open is the
minimum honest convention.

### C. Bearish thesis reversed

The opening PEs were directionally right until the 22,559 morning low, then
the market rallied ~190 points. The 10:05 strong PE was a fresh failure. The
day is a reminder that the strategy can be stopped out by a reversal even
after a correct early read; four trades is far too small a sample to judge
the edge.

### D. Replay gap

The cost-aware replay engine models caps, cooldown, exits and costs, but it
does **not** yet model the current re-entry policy (`no-new-information`,
+2/flip, two-loss pause). A warm-up test in the replay therefore measures the
entry-time filter in isolation, not the full production policy. The 2025
reconstructed replay also cannot exercise the opening gate: its first
actionable signal is **09:57**, so the 09:16 live entries have no historical
analogue. Exact snapshot replay and multi-symbol replay remain the next
layers.

## Pre-registered replay: warm-up gate + chase guard

*Written before running the test; the production daemon is unchanged.*

### Hypotheses

1. **H1 — 5-minute warm-up.** Starting entries at 09:20 instead of 09:15
   avoids the opening price-discovery bar and improves cost-aware net P&L.
2. **H2 — 15-minute warm-up.** Starting at 09:30 improves further, or
   over-delays and gives up too much of the session.
3. **H3 — chase guard.** Skipping an entry when the next 1-minute option bar
   opens more than 2% above the signal-bar close avoids chasing extended
   premiums and improves net P&L.

### Data and configuration

- **Data:** `thetrademarkk/india-index-options-1m` (HF, CC-BY-NC-4.0),
  NIFTY index + option 1-minute bars; third-party research data.
- **Window:** **2025-07-01 .. 2025-08-29** (42 sessions). The initial
  choice was 2026-06-03 .. 2026-07-02, but the HF option files for
  2026-06-16/23/30 are **missing** and 2026-06-09 is a 32 KB stub, so the
  replay would have selected the 2026-07-07 expiry for most of June — not
  the production weekly selection. The 2025 window has complete weekly
  expiries and was not used in the Jan–May 2026 calibration. This amendment
  was made **before any arm was run**. Results are a first slice, not proof.
- **Config:** current daemon settings — 8 per symbol / 24 total, 300 s exit
  cooldown, max profit/loss 20/15, trail 5, `minConfidence: moderate`,
  multi-timeframe filter on, ATM (`otmSkip: 0`), last entry 15:15,
  square-off 15:15 (conservative replay setting; daemon default 15:35),
  10 bps/side slippage, statutory costs.
- **Fills:** next 1-minute option bar open; stop-first intrabar ordering; no
  look-ahead; zero-DTE expiry skipped; max strike distance 3%.
- **Re-entry policy:** not modelled in this slice (see gap D).

### Metrics and decision rule

- **Primary:** net P&L per arm over the window.
- **Secondary:** trades, net win rate, net profit factor, max closed-trade
  drawdown, daily net series; paired daily bootstrap vs A0 (90% CI).
- **Promote only if:** (a) net improvement vs A0 is positive with a 90%
  bootstrap CI excluding zero, (b) max drawdown is no worse than 1.25× A0,
  and (c) the arm has at least 30 trades. Otherwise record a negative or
  inconclusive result and leave production unchanged.
- Even a pass requires a second fresh window or forward paper confirmation
  before any paper-only rule change. **Live arming remains out of scope.**

**Result: inconclusive for H1–H3; negative for the baseline edge.**

- **The warm-up gate never binds in this dataset.** A1/A2 are identical to A0
  because the earliest actionable entry signal across all 42 sessions is
  **09:57 IST** (minute 597; `warmupBlocked: 0`). The live 2026-10-22
  entries at 09:15:59–09:16:32 came from the **live stale-gate clearing**,
  which the reconstructed historical signal path does not reproduce. The gate
  is implemented and unit-tested; this window cannot exercise it.
- **The 2% chase guard never binds either.** `premiumMoveBlocked: 0` across
  190 entries: no next 1-minute option bar opened more than 2% above the
  signal-bar close. The 2026-10-22 FINNIFTY case (+11% next-bar open) is
  exactly the opening-auction gap the reconstructed replay lacks. A
  diagnostic run at 0.01% on 2025-07-01..04 blocked 18 entries, proving the
  guard is wired; that diagnostic is not evidence and is not a threshold to
  promote.
- **Macro/VIX parity did not change the result.** Coverage was 0/42 before
  fetching the 2025 proxies and 42/42 after; net P&L and trade count were
  identical, so the V3/VIX layers did not alter this window's trades.
- **The baseline is negative even before costs.** 190 trades, 31.6% win, net
  −₹24,063.28 (−24.06% on ₹1 lakh), charges ₹11,568.28, slippage
  ₹2,803.51, max DD 24.06%. Adding back the ₹14,371.79 charges and slippage
  leaves −₹9,691.49 pre-cost. This is a negative cost-aware result consistent
  with the earlier replay evidence; it does not support live arming.
- **Decision per pre-registration:** **no arm passed** (no improvement; the
  gate/guard did not bind). Production stays unchanged. The warm-up and
  fill-guard hypotheses remain **untested**, not rejected. The next test must
  use exact snapshot replay for the live sessions (where 09:16 entries exist)
  and model the re-entry policy; the reconstructed historical replay is the
  wrong instrument for the opening-time question.
- **Caveats:** 2025 regime, 42 sessions, NIFTY-only, third-party 1-minute
  OHLC, no bid/ask, re-entry policy not modelled. The negative baseline is
  not a verdict on the current daemon's exact configuration, but it is
  another reason not to arm live.
