## What didn't work / risks

### A. Lot-size bug — fixed in this change
Every order used NIFTY's old 65-unit lot. BN/FIN quantities were wrong, the
headline was overstated, and live BN/FIN orders would likely have been rejected
or mis-sized because 65 is not a multiple of 30 or 65. The selector now takes
the underlying symbol, the lot map uses 75 / 30 / 65, and `OrderGateway` rejects
any quantity that is not a whole multiple of the contract lot.

### B. NIFTY churn
Five valid NIFTY trades in 22 minutes, four losses and one scratch. The
60-second cooldown allowed repeated re-entry into the same chop. Two trades
reached +5.7–6.1% MFE and still ended at breakeven after giving back the
trailing-stop distance. This is a hypothesis to test, not a reason to change
exits from one day; the prior exit grid already failed.

### C. Correlated, one-direction book
All eight trades were PE. BN and FIN entered simultaneously; NIFTY would have
joined them at 12:33 if the cap had room. There is no max-concurrent or
same-direction exposure cap. With 8/18, three simultaneous PE positions are
possible, which is effectively one macro bet. The corrected concurrent
exposure at 12:33 was about **₹25,067 (26.7% of day-start equity)**.

### D. Paper/live divergence
- Paper fills at LTP; no bid/ask, statutory costs or realistic slippage.
- Paper does not enforce sentiment faults; live would likely have skipped the
  first trade because of stale VIX.
- The daemon has **no separate live cap**: 8/18 applies to both modes. If
  tighter live caps are intended, they still need implementation.
- The old ledger was not live-representative because of the 65-unit sizing.

### E. Cap increase untested
The 8/18 change came at 15:01, after the signal had faded. The 19 strong NIFTY
readings were all under the old cap. Today provides **no evidence** for or
against 8/18.

### F. Intraday restarts and snapshot bloat
Six restarts after the initial start; the 12:21 restart caused the seed entry.
Prefer deployments outside market hours or only while flat with fail-closed
verified. The DB also collected 1,289 cap-blocked NIFTY rows because the
fingerprint includes spot; throttling blocked-state snapshots would reduce
noise.

### G. Post-close health
The feed's 26-minute message age after 15:30 is expected. Do not treat it as an
outage; the data is simply not ticking after market close.

6. **Live readiness:** remain disarmed. Fix/verify separate live caps if
   intended, reconcile paper/live sentiment gates, and resolve the existing

7. **Ops:** keep the fail-closed and 45s watchdog changes; monitor ltt across
